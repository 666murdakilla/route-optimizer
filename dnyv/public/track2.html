Track 2 · Verification by Experience

You weren't born here. Prove you became a New Yorker.

Track 1 verifies New Yorkers by origin. Track 2 is for everyone else: the ones who arrived, stayed, paid for it, and became part of what makes this city so special. Answer honestly. The Department keeps score.

This is a petition, not a quiz. Check only what is true. A Verifier reads every file by hand; the running tally on the right is the Department's, for its own use, and it is watching three things above all — how long you have been here, how well you know the ground, and what the city has cost you. Each has a floor you must clear on its own; points elsewhere will not carry you past it.
